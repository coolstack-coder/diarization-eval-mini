How this is scored

  • DER, no collar, overlap scored, system's own VAD
  • Speaker count not given (see other tab for hinted)
  • Macro average over 7 domains, equal weight
  • Paired bootstrap over recordings, 10k resamples
  • Word-level outputs → segments (gap < 0.5 s), open converter
  • Failures = 100% missed speech, counted publicly
